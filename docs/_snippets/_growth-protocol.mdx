:::note[Overnight culture — shared protocol]
1. Inoculate 5 mL LB + appropriate antibiotic from a single colony.
2. Grow overnight at 37 °C, 200 rpm.
3. Sub-culture 1:100 into fresh medium; induce at OD₆₀₀ ≈ 0.4.
:::
