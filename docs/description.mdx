## The problem

Alzheimer's disease is typically diagnosed years after the underlying neurodegeneration
begins. Earlier, cheaper, less invasive signals are needed.

## Our approach

**Synwave** is an engineered bacterial biosensor that reads the **gut–brain axis** for
early biomarkers associated with Alzheimer's disease.

- **Sense** — recognise target metabolites in the gut environment.
- **Process** — a genetic logic layer filters signal from noise.
- **Report** — produce a measurable readout.

## Why it matters

A non-invasive, low-cost screen could move detection upstream of symptom onset, where
intervention is most valuable.
